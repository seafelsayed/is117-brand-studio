Behind this direction

Reference: Martine Bedin, Super lamp prototype, 1981, made by Fausto Celati for Memphis. V&A: What is Postmodernism? Photograph inspected: blue semicircle, wheels, and colored bulb holders that make a lamp resemble a playful wheeled object.

  • An arched product backdrop quotes the semicircular silhouette without reproducing the lamp.
  • Serif and sans-serif type mix editorial and everyday visual languages.
  • Offset frames and overlapping shapes challenge the single rigid grid while leaving the text upright and readable.

Intended effect: Suggest that familiar everyday pieces can be reinterpreted in a personal way.

Risk: The layered composition might feel busier than the relaxed brand promise.
