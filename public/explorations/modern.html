Behind this direction

Reference: Christian Dell, Dell-Lampe Type K, 1929, made by Zimmermann GmbH. V&A: What was Modernism? Photograph inspected: curved shade, upright support, circular base, visibly functional joints.

  • A consistent grid orders reading, product, and action by function.
  • One sans-serif family and aligned edges create a clear hierarchy.
  • The exposed geometric frame translates the lamp’s legible construction into a repeatable web layout.

Intended effect: Reduce outfit-planning pressure through a clear, calm starting point.

Risk: The ordered presentation could feel too impersonal for students seeking self-expression.
